Screen rules

How erbrus reads an agent's terminal: RE2 patterns, one per line, matched in multi-line mode against the bottom 15 lines of the screen (so ^ and $ bound a line and \n can join two). Order: working → idle → needs input. Any non-empty list replaces all built-in rules for that provider; leave all three empty to go back to built-ins.
